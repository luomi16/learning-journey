## Start here
This is an instructional example, not a reading record. The date is a demonstration date, and example.com links are placeholders. Replace the titles, authors, links, and takeaways with your own material.

Copy templates/day.md into content/ with a date-based filename. Set title to a theme for the day and date to the day you read the papers. Add another Paper and Takeaways pair for each additional reading; keep one Outputs section at the end.

## Make it yours
Use short takeaways on the timeline. Put explanations, comparisons, and open questions in extra sections here or in a separate note. Create reusable concept pages in knowledge/ and link keywords to them.

Replace Published with a publication date or year. Leave it blank or remove the line if you do not know it. The reading date comes from date at the top of this file.

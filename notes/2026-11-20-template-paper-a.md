## Create a day
Copy templates/day.md to content/2026-11-21.md. Replace the filename and date with your reading date, and give the day a short English title. Each date appears as one timeline card.

## Add your readings
For each paper, write a Paper section with its full title and original URL, followed by a Takeaways section. Repeat this pair for every paper you read that day.

Use the headings `#### Paper` and `#### Takeaways`. Remove the second pair when you read only one paper. Add new pairs before the day's Outputs section.

## Record paper details
Write Authors and Published directly below the paper link. Use the author names from the paper and its publication date or year. Remove either line when the information is unknown; the paper list will show Not recorded.

Read papers automatically collects these details. Its Read on field uses the day's date, so you do not have to enter your reading date twice.

## Write useful takeaways
- Capture one idea per bullet in your own words.
- Include a condition, limitation, or question when it matters.
- Use bold text for emphasis and links for ideas you want to revisit.

To link a keyword, write `[keyword](knowledge/template-concept)`. Open the [knowledge-page example](knowledge/template-concept) to see how a reusable concept page works.

## Collect outputs
Write Outputs once, after all the paper and takeaway pairs. Link actual notes, code, diagrams, or experiments. Keep longer explanations in [a detailed note](notes/2026-11-20-template-paper-b) or in extra sections below the daily summary.

## Preview your changes
Save your files, run npm run build from the project folder, and refresh the local preview. Keep templates/ as your blank starting point; files in that folder are not published.

## Definition
Replace this paragraph with a clear explanation of one concept in your own words. State the problem it helps you understand and define any unfamiliar terms.

## A simple example
Add a concrete example that will make the concept easy to recall. Keep the example focused on the main idea.

## Assumptions and limits
Describe when the idea applies, what it assumes, and where your understanding is still incomplete.

## Connections and sources
Link papers, notes, or other concept pages that helped you understand the idea. Explain what each connection contributes.

A keyword in a takeaway can point here using `[keyword](knowledge/template-concept)`. Multiple readings can reference the same page; update the concept once as your understanding improves.

## Open questions
List what you want to verify or learn next. Replace these instructions with your own questions when you start using the page.

## Create your own page
Copy templates/knowledge.md to knowledge/your-concept-name.md. Set its title, replace the example date with your learning date, and fill in the sections. Leave out the date to keep the page in the final Undated group. Then use `[keyword](knowledge/your-concept-name)` wherever that concept appears.

Use a stable filename with letters, numbers, hyphens, or underscores. Create the file before linking to it. For more examples, open the [daily writing guide](notes/2026-11-20-template-paper-a).
